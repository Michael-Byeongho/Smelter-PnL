Methodology and interpretation

Required weighted TC = (cash cost + target cash margin − non-TCRC revenue) ÷ TCRC-to-TC factor. Implied spot/index TC = (required weighted TC − benchmark share × benchmark TC) ÷ (1 − benchmark share). Smelter cash margin = non-TCRC revenue + weighted TC × TCRC-to-TC factor − cash cost. The conversion factor captures the assumed contribution of refining charges.
